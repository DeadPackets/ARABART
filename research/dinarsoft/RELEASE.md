# Freeware release of DinarSoft software

On 2026-10-22 Yasser Awad, DinarSoft's founder and the author of its software, agreed to distribute all DinarSoft
applications as freeware. His son relayed the decision in the build session ("yasser agreed to distribute as freeware").

Scope: every DinarSoft application and version that survives (MemMaid, HandySwitcher, HandyMenu, HandyLauncher, TapText,
HijriDate). The site may host the original installers and publish what is needed to unlock the full versions (the unlock
key method, working keys or a key generator), credited to the Internet Archive for the recovered files.

Site wording must not invent a quote from him; state it as his decision ("In 2026, Yasser Awad released...").
