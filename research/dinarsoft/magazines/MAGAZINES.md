# DinarSoft in print magazines

Pages from print magazines (and print-layout PDF editions) that name DinarSoft or one of its apps. Searched 2026-10-23.

**Result: 18 print mentions (issues or reports) in 7 magazine titles, PC Magazine included (May 2008, page 82).** The others are Smartphone & Pocket PC (Thaddeus Computing, 5 issues plus a special report), Computer Power User (1), PC Today (2), CHIP Russia (4), Hard'n'Soft, Russia (2) and Popsoft, China (2).

Paths:

- Raw full pages (git-ignored): `research/dinarsoft/magazines/raw/<slug>/`
- Lossless crops: `research/dinarsoft/magazines/crops/<name>.png`
- Published crops: `public/dinarsoft/press/<name>.webp`, same pixel size as the PNG

Every crop name below exists in both places. Sizes are width x height in pixels.

Scan types:

- **Paper scan**: a photo or scan of a printed page.
- **Digital edition**: the publisher's print-layout PDF, as uploaded to the Internet Archive. It shows the same page as print. Red boxes in some crops are PDF link frames, not ink.

### Crop review

I viewed each crop. All are readable and straight.

Excluded on purpose:

- A judge's personal email on Buyer's Guide 2006 page 69.
- A third-party watermark with a personal email across Buyer's Guide 2007 pages 56, 61 and 63. The crops avoid it.
- The "YYePG" release line on Apr/May 2006 page 81.
- The issue's download code on CHIP May 2007.
- The editor's email on the Popsoft pages.
- Print-shop slugs on CHIP June 2008.

No crop shows a personal phone number, street address or personal email.

Covers: CHIP Russia scans start at the editor's letter, and the Hard'n'Soft July 2008 scan starts at the contents page. Those issues use a `--dateline` crop in place of a cover.
